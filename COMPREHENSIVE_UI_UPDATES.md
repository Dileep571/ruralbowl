## 🎨 Design System

### Colors:
- Primary: Green (#16a34a to #15803d)
- Accent: Various gradients
- Background: Gray-50 (#f9fafb)
- White: #ffffff

### Components:
- Rounded corners: `rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`
- Shadows: `shadow-md` hover to `shadow-xl`
- Transitions: `transition-all duration-300`
- Hover effects: `hover:-translate-y-2` or `hover:scale-105`

### Icons:
- Use `lucide-react` throughout
- Size: Usually `w-5 h-5` or `w-6 h-6`
- Color: Match design context
